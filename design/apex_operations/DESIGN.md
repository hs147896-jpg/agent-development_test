## Brand & Style

This design system translates high-performance automotive engineering aesthetics into a mission-critical cafe operations management platform. The brand personality is uncompromisingly precise, technical, focused, and austere. It treats cafe workflows—espresso yield ratios, inventory replenishment cadences, peak-hour kitchen ticket throughput, and equipment telemetry—with the rigour of motorsport track telemetry.

The target audience encompasses multi-unit operators, head baristas, kitchen commanders, and operational directors who require instant situational awareness without decorative distractions. The interface evokes a sense of calculated control, mechanical reliability, and velocity. 

The stylistic approach merges **Brutalist Precision** with **Minimalist High-Contrast Ergonomics**:
- **Monolithic Dark Surface**: Absolute blacks (`#000000`) and low-reflectance charcoal tiers anchor the operator's attention during fast-paced shifts under harsh cafe lighting.
- **Strict Rectilinear Discipline**: Pure right angles (`0px` border radii) establish architectural stability across modular kitchen display systems, inventory tables, and extraction matrices.
- **Engineered Status Telemetry**: Color is withheld from purely decorative uses. Chromatic saturation is strictly reserved for machine telemetry, status differentials (M-Blue, Electric Blue, Warning Gold, Red Accent), and the tri-color operational indicator.
- **Wireframe Lightness**: Interface chrome relies on razor-thin 1px structural hairlines (`#3c3c3c` and `#262626`), eliminating drop shadows and ambient visual haze entirely.

## Colors

The system operates exclusively within a pitch-dark operational paradigm designed for low light fatigue and extreme contrast readability.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#000000`)**: The terminal floor. Absolute black ensures optimal contrast against OLED kitchen displays and eliminates perimeter bleeding.
- **Operational White (`#ffffff`)**: Serves as the primary functional trigger and dominant headline ink. It commands absolute visual authority across buttons, primary metrics, and active states.
- **Tonal Stepping Surfaces**:
  - `surface-soft` (`#0d0d0d`): Neutral table striping, inactive lanes, and floor-level metrics.
  - `surface-card` (`#1a1a1a`): Primary modular container background for ticket orders, recipe cards, and bean hopper readouts.
  - `surface-elevated` (`#262626`): Nested modules, active modal sheets, and header banners.
  - `carbon-gray` (`#2b2b2b`): High-friction telemetry cards, equipment status panels, and sensor data arrays.

### Hairlines & Boundaries
- Structural boundaries and dividers default strictly to 1px `#3c3c3c` (`hairline`).
- Nested interior dividers use `#262626` (`hairline-strong`) to segment data cells without fracturing the container's structural integrity.

### Engineered Status Accents
Chromatic accents are deployment-specific telemetry indicators:
- **`m-blue-light` (`#0066b1`) & `m-blue-dark` (`#1c69d4`)**: Routine machine cycles, connected grinders, batch brewing processes, and calibrated baseline metrics.
- **`electric-blue` (`#0653b6`)**: Automated continuous telemetry, real-time IoT water filtration sensors, and smart scale inputs.
- **`warning-gold` (`#f4b400`)**: Hopper depletion warnings, temp variances > 1.5°C, milk inventory thresholds, and delayed order warnings.
- **`m-red` (`#e22718`)**: Emergency pump failure, grinder jamming, ticket timers past 6 minutes, and voided orders.
- **`success-green` (`#0fa336`)**: Completed orders, calibrated dial-in confirmation, and verified sanitation cycles.

*Rule: The signature tri-stripe accent (`m-blue-light` → `m-blue-dark` → `m-red`) is exclusively deployed as a structural divider or system state banner; it must never be used as a plain button background.*

## Typography

Typography delivers brutal clarity, stark hierarchy, and instant legibility across ambient kitchen distances. Using **Hanken Grotesk**, the system establishes high tension between solid structural display headers (700 weight) and featherweight, technical data points (300 weight).

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Architecture**: Display sizes (`display-xl` down to `title-lg`) are set at 700 weight with tight line-heights (1.0 to 1.15). Use them for top-level operational counters, daily gross extraction yields, and station status alerts.
- **Body & Data Rendering**: Body text uses 300 weight to prevent visual clustering. Numerical metrics, table entries, and order summaries are cleanly delineated without bloat.
- **Uppercase Discipline**: Operational tags, table headers, column categorizations, and interactive actions strictly utilize `label-uppercase` or `label-button` with an expanded letter-spacing (`0.1em`).
- **Responsive Downscaling**: Screen sizes below `768px` automatically step `display-xl` down to `48px` and `display-lg` down to `36px` to maintain single-line integrity across mobile service handhelds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an engineered telemetry cockpit: a strictly compartmentalized 12-column grid system optimized for high-density information architecture.

### Grid & Composition Models
- **Standard Frame**: Desktop views are bounded within an ultra-wide fixed canvas threshold of `1440px`, flanked by `margin` (`2.5rem`). Fluid 100% full-bleed layouts are reserved for real-time station flow diagrams and full-width station telemetry bands.
- **Columns & Gutters**:
  - **Desktop (`≥ 1024px`)**: 12-column structural layout with `gutter` (`1.5rem`) for 3-up ticket blocks, 4-up group-head telemetry modules, and multi-station production matrices.
  - **Tablet (`768px – 1023px`)**: 6-column layout with `gutter` (`1rem`) providing 2-up split screen monitoring (Espresso Line + Batch Filter).
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Single-column vertical stream, edge-to-edge modular cards separated by `gutter-mobile` (`0.75rem`) and `margin-mobile` (`1rem`).

### Spacing Rhythm
Built on an immutable 4px/8px baseline rhythm:
- `space-xxs` (4px): Height of the signature tri-color stripe indicator; micro icon alignments.
- `space-xs` (8px): Form input gaps, pill-switch internals, tight metadata pairings.
- `space-sm` (12px): Standard cell padding in high-density inventory spreadsheets; button vertical inner padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, action button horizontal offsets, navigation gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard margin between distinct monitoring modules and multi-column gaps.
- `space-xl` (40px) & `space-xxl` (64px): Section boundaries for shift change summaries and high-level analytical breakdowns.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic drop shadows, skeuomorphic glows, and translucent blur filters. Visual depth is rendered completely flat, achieved through **tonal surface stacking** and **1px structural hairlines**.

### Surface Tiering
1. **Ground Zero (`#000000`)**: The foundational canvas plane. Base background for the overall application and primary navigation strips.
2. **First Tier (`#0d0d0d` to `#1a1a1a`)**: Operational cards, ticket units, and data modules. Bounded by a crisp 1px `#3c3c3c` hairline outline.
3. **Second Tier (`#262626`)**: Active ticket selection, focused inspection modals, and flyout configuration trays.
4. **Textural Focus (`#2b2b2b`)**: Reserved for physical hardware readouts (boiler pressure, extraction yield scales, cold storage temperatures).

### Spatial Boundaries
- **No Diffusion**: Shadows evaluate to `box-shadow: none`. Surfaces meet via razor-sharp, zero-radius mechanical intersections.
- **Hairline Framing**: Every elevated panel, table cell, and modal dialog is delineated by a 1px solid border (`#3c3c3c` or `#262626`).
- **Focus Activation**: When an interactive tile or input field receives focus, its perimeter switches instantaneously to a solid 1px `#ffffff` hairline, cutting through the dark surroundings with high clarity.

## Shapes

The shape system enforces an architectural binary: **absolute rectangles (`0px`)** versus **engineered circular controls (`9999px`)**.

### Sharp Rectilinear System (`roundedness: 0`)
All foundational interface architecture is strictly non-rounded:
- **Buttons (Primary, Secondary, Ghost)**: 0px border radius.
- **Cards, Kitchen Tickets & Containers**: 0px border radius.
- **Data Tables & Spec Cells**: 0px border radius.
- **Form Inputs & Checkboxes**: 0px border radius.

This pure geometric geometry reflects machined metal parts, portafilters, and digital commercial kitchen equipment.

### Circular Utility Overrides
Roundness is strictly isolated to utility controls requiring omnidirectional action:
- **Circle Action Controls (`rounded.full` / `9999px`)**: Sized to an exact 48px × 48px bounding box with a 1px `#3c3c3c` outline (e.g., carousel arrows, quick-increment buttons, dial-in fine adjusters).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (`button-primary`)**: Solid white fill (`#ffffff`), pitch black text (`#000000`), 0px border radius, 14px bold uppercase text with `0.1em` letter spacing. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover: inverts to `#e6e6e6` with zero transition lag.
- **Secondary Action (`button-outline`)**: Transparent fill, 1px solid white outline (`#ffffff`), crisp white text (`#ffffff`), 0px border radius. Hover: background fills with `#1a1a1a`.
- **Destructive Action (`button-destructive`)**: Transparent fill, 1px solid `#e22718`, red text (`#e22718`). Hover: solid red fill with white text.
- **Icon Buttons (`button-icon`)**: 48px × 48px square or circular (`9999px`), 1px solid `#3c3c3c` boundary, centered monochromatic glyph.

### Kitchen Order Display (KOD) & Metric Cards
- **Structure**: Rectangular cards (`#1a1a1a`), 0px border radius, outlined by 1px solid `#3c3c3c`. Padding: `24px`.
- **Telemetry Header**: Order ID or machine label set in `label-uppercase` (`#7e7e7e`), accompanied by a live timer.
- **Priority Indicator**: Critical or delayed orders feature a 4px vertical bar on the left perimeter colored in `#e22718`. Normal orders use `#0066b1`.

### Data Tables & Spec Cells
- **Header Cells**: Height: 44px, background: `#0d0d0d`, text: `label-uppercase` (`#7e7e7e`), bottom border: 1px solid `#3c3c3c`.
- **Data Rows**: Height: 48px, background: `#000000` (alternating with `#0d0d0d` on high-density grids), text: `body-sm` (`#ffffff`), bottom border: 1px solid `#262626`.
- **Status Cells**: Right-aligned, featuring a solid 6px status square next to light-weight monospace numbers.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height: 48px, background: `#1a1a1a`, border: 1px solid `#3c3c3c`, text: 16px `#ffffff` (300 weight), placeholder: `#7e7e7e`, 0px border radius. Padding: `12px 16px`. Focus state: border switches to 1px solid `#ffffff`.
- **Checkboxes**: 20px × 20px square, 0px border radius, 1px solid `#7e7e7e`, background: transparent. Checked state: solid white fill with centered black checkmark glyph.
- **Radio Buttons**: 20px × 20px outer circle, 1px solid `#7e7e7e`. Selected state: central concentric 8px solid white circle.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Structure**: Compact, 0px border radius, 1px solid hairline border. Padding: `4px 8px`. Font: `caption` in bold uppercase.
- **Variants**:
  - `Running / Nominal`: Background `#0d0d0d`, border `#0fa336`, text `#0fa336`.
  - `Dialing In / Calibrating`: Background `#0d0d0d`, border `#1c69d4`, text `#1c69d4`.
  - `Attention Required`: Background `#0d0d0d`, border `#f4b400`, text `#f4b400`.
  - `Critical Error`: Background `#0d0d0d`, border `#e22718`, text `#e22718`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Extraction Pressure & Yield Gauge**: Textural carbon panel (`#2b2b2b`) with 1px border (`#3c3c3c`). Large 32px bold real-time readout in `#ffffff`, baseline label in `label-uppercase`, and a 2px horizontal electric-blue (`#0653b6`) progress stroke.
- **Brand Signature Accent Line (`m-stripe-divider`)**: A 4px high horizontal divider splitting into equal thirds of `#0066b1` (Light Blue) → `#1c69d4` (Dark Blue) → `#e22718` (Red). Deployed at the top of main dashboard views, shift changeovers, and station switchers.
